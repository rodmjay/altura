Altura · a life reset retreat · Costa Rica

Come for a week. Leave with your own Should.

Should is a team of AI agents that runs the admin of your life: email, calendar, to-dos, bills and goals. At Altura you spend a week in Costa Rica's Central Valley setting up your own, on your own laptop, alongside professionals from around the world. You fly home with it running, and with a network that lasts.

What you take home

What Should does all day

Should already runs one person's life, every day. It handles the small stuff on its own and brings you only the decisions that are really yours.

  • sms

    One morning text

    At 8 AM: the three things that matter today, what you're waiting on, and anything that needs you. Nothing else.

  • groups

    An agent team for the small stuff

    Agents for money, home, health, career and travel sort the inbox, track bills, file the paperwork and watch prices, so you don't have to remember.

  • touch_app

    Only the real decisions reach you

    Anything that spends money, sends a message to someone or can't be undone waits for your yes. You answer with 1, 2 or 3 from your phone.

  • dashboard

    A dashboard for everything

    Every agent has a desk on a private dashboard for your laptop and phone, with your projects and goals beside them. It can show on the TV at home too.

  • explore

    It starts from your values

    Your values and goals decide the small calls. A weekly sit-down keeps them current and turns the week into at most three commitments.

Where it is today

Honest about what this is

  • A working prototype

    Should runs its builder's life every day. It isn't a finished app you download, and it changes every week.

  • Set up by hand, with you

    At Altura we build your copy together, step by step, around your accounts and your rules. Expect a few rough edges.

  • No promised outcomes

    It can take a lot of small work off your plate. It won't fix your money, health or career for you, and we don't promise income or results.

Your data

  • laptop_mac

    Your Should runs on your machine, with your own Claude account. Altura never holds your data.

  • link

  • rule

The week

From what matters to you, to it running

Mornings are for building, afternoons are for the valley. A draft plan for the pilot, so details will change.

  1. Sunday

    Arrive

    Pickup at the San José airport, the pool and gardens, a welcome dinner. A quick laptop check so everyone starts ready.

  2. Monday

    Values and goals

    A sit-down on what matters to you: your values, a few goals for the year, and what good looks like in each part of life. Then we lock down your accounts with a password manager, two-factor sign-in and a credit freeze. Dinner ends with a problem swap.

  3. Tuesday

    Connect your accounts

    Claude Code in plain words, then your email, calendar and to-dos connected. The inbox gets cleaned up, everything goes into one list, and your morning text is scheduled.

  4. Wednesday

    Rest

    Poás volcano at sunrise, hot springs or a slow day. Your first morning text arrives wherever you are.

  5. Thursday

    Choose your agents

    Pick the agents you want first, from money and bills to home and paper mail, and write the rules each one follows.

  6. Friday

    Teach them your rules

    Health, career and travel agents, then one-on-one walks with the group. At demo night everyone shows one agent in two minutes.

  7. Saturday

    Go home with it running

    Your first weekly review, together, and a 90-day plan. A last security check, then the shuttle to the airport. The cohort chat stays open after you leave.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You type plain requests to Claude, and we do the setup with you, step by step.

Am I buying Should?

No. Should is a working prototype. At Altura you set up your own copy of the same approach on your own laptop and accounts, and nothing of yours runs on our side.

What does it cost?

Dates and price aren't set yet; the pilot list helps us set them. Your Claude plan is separate and paid to Anthropic.

Who else will be there?

A small group of professionals from different countries and fields. The pilot list helps us shape the group, and you'll know who's coming before you fly.

I use ChatGPT. Can I still come?

Should is built on Claude and Claude Code today, so you'll need a Claude Pro or Max account for the week.

Can I see it first?

Yes. The public demo shows the dashboard with made-up data, and the Should page explains how it works.
