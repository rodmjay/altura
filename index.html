Altura · a life reset retreat · Costa Rica

Come for a week. Set up your own Should.

Should is a team of AI agents that runs the admin of your life: email, calendar, to-dos, bills and goals. Altura is the week you set up your own, on your own laptop and your own Claude account, in Costa Rica's Central Valley, alongside a small group of professionals we're inviting from around the world. You go home with a setup built around your life, and a cohort chat that stays open.

What you take home

What Should does all day

Should already runs one person's life, every day. It handles the small stuff on its own and brings you only the decisions that are really yours.

  • One morning summary

    At 8 AM, in chat: the three things that matter today, what you're waiting on, and anything that needs you. By text too, once you add your own number.

  • An agent team for the small stuff

    Agents for money, home, health, career and travel sort the inbox, track bills, file the paperwork and watch prices, so you don't have to remember.

  • Only the real decisions reach you

    Anything that spends money, sends a message to someone or can't be undone waits for your yes. You answer with 1, 2 or 3.

  • A dashboard for everything

    Every agent has a desk on a private dashboard for your laptop and phone, with your projects and goals beside them.

  • It starts from your values

    Your values and goals decide the small calls. A weekly sit-down keeps them current and turns the week into at most three commitments.

Where it is today

Honest about what this is

  • A working prototype

    Should runs its builder's life every day. It isn't a finished app you download, and it changes every week.

  • Set up by hand, with you

    At Altura we build your copy together, step by step, around your accounts and your rules. Expect a few rough edges.

  • No promised outcomes

    It can take a lot of small work off your plate. It won't fix your money, health or career for you, and we don't promise income or results.

Your data

Your life stays on your machine

  • Your data stays with you

    Your Should runs on your machine, with your own Claude account. Altura never stores what your Should holds. We help at your screen when you ask, and never type or keep your passwords.

  • Your accounts, your access

    You connect the email, calendar and to-do apps you already use, and you choose what each agent may read.

  • Rules you write

    Agents ask before money, messages to other people, deletes and settings. You decide what else they ask about.

The week

From what matters to you, to your own setup

Mornings are for building, afternoons are for the valley. A draft plan for the pilot, so details will change.

  1. Sunday

    Arrive

    Pickup at the San José airport, the pool and gardens, a welcome dinner. A quick laptop check so everyone starts ready.

  2. Monday

    Values and goals

    A sit-down on what matters to you: your values, what never happens without your yes, what's never shared, and what a good week looks like. Then you lock down your accounts with a password manager, two-factor sign-in and a credit freeze, with us beside you.

  3. Tuesday

    Connect your accounts

    Claude Code in plain words, then your email, calendar and to-dos connected. The inbox gets cleaned up, everything goes into one list, and your morning summary is scheduled.

  4. Wednesday

    Rest

    Poás volcano at sunrise, hot springs or a slow day. Your first morning summary is waiting wherever you are.

  5. Thursday

    Choose your agents

    Pick the agents you want first, from money and bills to home and paper mail, and write the rules each one follows.

  6. Friday

    Teach them your rules

    Health, career and travel agents, then one-on-one walks with the group. At demo night everyone shows one agent in two minutes.

  7. Saturday

    Take it home

    Your first weekly review, together, and a 90-day plan. A last security check, then the shuttle to the airport. The cohort chat stays open after you leave.

The people

Meet professionals from around the world

Altura is a small group of professionals, each building their own Should, and we're inviting them from around the world. The week is built so you get to know them.

  • A small group, open to the world

    We're inviting people from different fields and, we hope, different countries and stages of life. Small enough that everyone knows everyone by Tuesday.

  • Long dinners and problem swaps

    Evenings are for long-table dinners and swapping problems with people who solve them differently than you do.

  • One-on-one walks

    Friday's midday walks pair you with someone new, through the coffee hills.

  • A network that lasts

    The cohort chat stays open after the week, so you keep comparing notes, setups and ideas from home.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You type plain requests to Claude, and we do the setup with you, step by step.

Am I buying Should?

No. Should is a working prototype. At Altura you set up your own copy of the same approach on your own laptop and accounts, and nothing of yours runs on our side.

What does it cost?

Dates and price aren't set yet; the pilot list helps us set them. Your Claude plan is separate and paid to Anthropic.

Who else will be there?

A small group of professionals from different fields, and we hope different countries. The pilot list helps us shape the group, and you'll know who's coming before you fly.

I use ChatGPT. Can I still come?

Should is built on Claude and Claude Code today, so you'll need a Claude Pro or Max account for the week.

Can I see it first?

Yes. The public demo shows the dashboard with made-up data, and the Should page explains how it works.
